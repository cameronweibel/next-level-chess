# ♟️ To-Do: Next Level Chess Redesign (Hormozi Framework)

This checklist provides a structured roadmap to redesign [Next Level Chess](https://nextlevelchess.com/) using high-converting principles.

---

## 📸 Phase 1: The "As-Is" Audit & Documentation

- [ ] **Full-Page Desktop Capture:** Take a high-resolution screenshot of the current homepage to establish a "before" baseline.
- [ ] **Mobile Layout Capture:** Screenshot the mobile experience to identify if the "Above the Fold" area is cluttered.
- [ ] **Course Page Audit:** Capture the [Simplified Chess Improvement System](https://courses.nextlevelchess.blog/) page to analyze current benefit stacking.
- [ ] **Identify Friction Points:** Mark every navigation link or button that doesn't lead directly to the "Most Wanted Action" (MWA).

## 🧠 Phase 2: Copywriting & Offer Strategy

- [ ] **Define the Dream Outcome:** Rewrite the main headline to focus on a specific result, such as "Reach 1200+ Elo in 30 Days".
- [ ] **Apply the Value Equation:** \* Increase **Dream Outcome** and **Perceived Likelihood** of success.
  - Decrease **Time Delay** and **Effort & Sacrifice** required by the student.
- [ ] **Draft the "Grand Slam" Headline:** Use a formula that solves a specific pain point, e.g., "Stop Blundering Your Games: The 3x3 Method to Consistent Wins".
- [ ] **Create the "So That" Stack:** List every feature followed by a benefit (e.g., "GM-led analysis _so that_ you never repeat the same mistake").
- [ ] **Build Scarcity & Urgency:** Add elements like "Only 5 coaching spots left" or a countdown for current course pricing.

## 🎨 Phase 3: High-Converting Design & Layout

- [ ] **Sticky Navbar:** Include the navigation bar from the main nextlevelchess.com site. It should be sticky, with the "NEXT LEVEL CHESS" brand on the left, center links for "Courses" (dropdown with "The Simplified Chess Improvement System" and "Reach 1200 Elo"), "Articles", and "About me", and a "Get my Free eBook" button on the right that smooth-scrolls to the hero email opt-in. Include a responsive mobile hamburger menu.
- [ ] **Implement Proven Layouts:** Choose a structure like "Headline + Image + 3 Bullet Points + Form".
- [ ] **Social Proof Wall:** Move the "5.0 star" ratings and specific student testimonials "above the fold".
- [ ] **Visual Hook:** Add a high-quality mockup of the "Simplified Chess Improvement System" or a video of GM Noël Studer teaching.
- [ ] **Contrast the CTA:** Ensure the "Buy Now" or "Get Free eBook" button is a high-contrast color that pops against the site’s dark theme.

## 🛠️ Phase 4: Lead Gen & Technical Setup

- [ ] **Optimize Lead Magnet:** Ensure the "Art of Chess Training" eBook is offered via a high-converting, single-field email opt-in.
- [ ] **Mobile Responsiveness:** Verify that chess diagrams and CTA buttons are easily tappable on small screens.
- [ ] **Install Exit-Intent Popup:** Add a "Wait! Don't leave your Elo behind" popup for users attempting to bounce.
- [ ] **Page Speed Check:** Compress all images to ensure the landing page loads in under 3 seconds.

## 🧪 Phase 5: Final Review & Testing

- [ ] **The "Stupid to Say No" Test:** Read the final offer aloud to ensure it feels like a "Grand Slam" that is hard to refuse.
- [ ] **Prepare A/B Tests:** Draft two headline variations (Outcome-based vs. Pain-based) to test after the initial launch.
- [ ] **Final Comparison:** Take an "After" screenshot to compare against the Phase 1 baseline for a cleaner, focused look.
